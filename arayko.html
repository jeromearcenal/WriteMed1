ABOUT WRITEMED

Health information that feels easier to understand.

Many people search several websites, ask family and friends, or visit pharmacies just for basic answers about common symptoms. Online results can be messy and confusing.

WriteMed brings general OTC medicine information together in one friendly, simple app. It was developed as a startup project in ISSPEC2 – Entrepreneurship at Davao del Norte State College.

◎

Our Mission

To make general OTC medicine information easier to access, simpler to understand, and safer to use.

◉

Our Vision

A community where everyone can find clear general medicine information and knows when to seek professional help.

HOW IT WORKS

Helpful information in three simple steps.

1

Search your symptom

Look up common concerns such as headache, fever, cough, colds, or body pain.

2

Read general info

See the general purpose, basic usage information, and safety reminders.

3

Find a pharmacy

Use the nearby pharmacy finder to locate pharmacies in your area.

FAQ

Common questions

Does WriteMed diagnose illnesses?

No. WriteMed provides general information only and does not diagnose illnesses or replace professional advice.

Can the app tell me which medicine to take?

No. Always follow the product label and ask a pharmacist or healthcare professional when you need advice.

Does the pharmacy finder show medicine stock?

No. It helps you find pharmacies near you but does not show or guarantee product availability.
